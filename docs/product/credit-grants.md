# Credit grants and ledger-linked buckets (LL-84)

Credit grants are optional provenance and policy for a credit wallet. A wallet is one ledger account with one posted balance. Every grant posts a balanced transaction: the funding account is debited and the wallet is credited. The ledger entries are the financial record. The grant row stores origin, policy, reference, and the issuance transaction ID; it stores no amount or remaining balance. An immutable link from the wallet entry to the grant identifies the issued lot.

Create a registered tenant asset first. The wallet must use that asset, have CREDIT side and DISALLOW overdraft policy, and start with zero posted and inflight balance. Its funding account must belong to the same tenant, ledger, and asset. The first grant converts the empty account to CREDIT_WALLET; this kind cannot revert. Ordinary accounts remain STANDARD. Generic postings and holds cannot touch CREDIT_WALLET accounts, so unattributed movements cannot invalidate a bucket balance. The adapter checks the account kind already returned by the account update; ordinary postings do not query grant tables.

POST /v1/credit-grants accepts ledger_id, account_id, funding_account_id, asset_id, reference, origin, amount_minor, policy, and optional external_reference. Origins are PURCHASED, PROMOTIONAL, TRIAL, and COMPENSATION. Purchased grants require refundable: true; promotional grants require refundable: false and transferable: false. Policy version 1 requires eligibility: null. New origins and executable eligibility rules require coordinated core, database, and HTTP contract changes. Amounts use positive signed-64-bit minor units of the registered asset. Legacy currency text is not the grant identity.

The (tenant_id, reference) grant key is unique. An identical retry returns the existing grant, comparing its amount from the issuance entry. A changed payload fails with CREDIT_GRANT_CONFLICT. The grant, transaction, and entry link commit atomically. Grant and link rows are append-only; wallet entries and their transactions cannot be updated or deleted. Composite foreign keys bind every link to the same tenant, ledger, wallet account, and underlying entry. A deferred database trigger rejects wallet entries without attribution at commit.

POST /v1/credit-grants/{id}/reversal fully compensates a grant only while its own capacity is untouched. It posts an opposite linked ledger transaction and links the wallet debit entry to the grant. Its tenant-scoped transaction reference is the reversal idempotency key; a second reference for the same grant conflicts. No separate reversal table or mutable grant status exists. Partial refunds and already-spent clawback policy belong to LL-87.

GET /v1/accounts/{id}/credit-balance returns one bucket per grant and separate origin_totals. Each bucket amount is derived from its linked ledger entries; the sum must equal both the account's posted balance and the sum of all posted wallet entries. Grant creation validates and locks the wallet but does not rescan prior grants or ledger entries. The balance read path checks financial totals, not every historical transaction's descriptive metadata. An explicit integrity audit may perform deeper history inspection separately. In this slice allocated, consumed, and expired are zero.

LL-66 will extend the write-time entry classification to link deductions to source grants and implement deterministic allocation, capacity locking, and lineage. LL-85 will extend it for expiration entries and enforce synchronous spend eligibility. The account remains the single wallet balance for historical queries, ownership linkage, and future period close. Credit grants remain optional: existing generic accounts and transactions keep their behavior. Migration 0012 is still unmerged; disposable databases that applied an earlier PR preview must be reset before using its revised schema.
