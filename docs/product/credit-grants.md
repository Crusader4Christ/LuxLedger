# Credit grants and buckets (LL-84)

A credit grant records why a wallet account received capacity. Every grant atomically posts a balanced transaction: the funding account is debited and the credit account is credited. The ledger entries remain the financial record. Grant rows carry immutable origin, policy, external reference, and posting linkage; they never replace double-entry transactions.

Create a registered tenant asset first. The wallet account must use that asset, have `CREDIT` side and `DISALLOW` overdraft policy, and start with zero posted and inflight balance. Its funding account must belong to the same tenant, ledger, and asset. Multiple grants may fund the same wallet account. Amounts are positive signed-64-bit integers in the asset's minor units. The grant's `asset_id` is authoritative; legacy currency strings are not used as grant identity.

`POST /v1/credit-grants` accepts `ledger_id`, `account_id`, `funding_account_id`, `asset_id`, `reference`, `origin`, `amount_minor`, `policy`, and optional `external_reference`. Origins are `PURCHASED`, `PROMOTIONAL`, `TRIAL`, and `COMPENSATION`. Purchased grants require `refundable: true`; promotional grants require `refundable: false` and `transferable: false`. Each grant records explicit `consumption_priority` and nullable `eligibility`. These fields describe policy; allocation and eligibility enforcement at spend time belong to LL-66. New origins require a versioned contract and database migration.

The `(tenant_id, reference)` grant key is unique. An identical retry returns the existing grant; any changed amount, origin, asset, account, policy, or external reference returns `CREDIT_GRANT_CONFLICT`. Concurrent requests serialize on that key. `POST /v1/credit-grants/{id}/reversal` fully compensates an unconsumed grant with an opposite linked transaction and an immutable reversal row. It is idempotent for the same reversal reference. A different reference or an already compensated grant fails. Partial refunds and adjustment workflows are not part of this slice.

`GET /v1/accounts/{id}/credit-balance` groups grants by origin. `granted_minor - reversed_minor = remaining_minor` in this slice; allocated, consumed, and expired are zero until LL-66 and LL-85 add their ledger-linked records. Each response verifies the grant and compensation entries and checks that bucket remaining amounts equal both the sum of all posted account entries and the account's cached balance. A mismatch fails with `CREDIT_GRANT_CONFLICT`; it is never silently presented as a valid bucket balance. Unattributed generic transactions and holds on a grant-backed wallet account are rejected by the adapter. Grant rows are append-only in PostgreSQL.

Existing generic ledger accounts and postings keep their current behavior. The migration adds new tables and does not backfill grants from old transactions, because their origin and policy cannot be inferred safely. To adopt credit buckets, create a dedicated zero-balance credit account and fund it with grants. Existing balances can be transferred through ordinary balanced postings before adoption, but those historical amounts are not automatically classified. LL-66 owns deterministic spend allocation and lineage; LL-85 owns expiration processing and time-bound eligibility.
